Epicranial E-Field Simulator

This tool models how electrical current from a 7-electrode epicranial array distributes through scalp, skull, CSF and cortex — targeting a bottom of suclus focal cortical dysplasia (FCD).

The solver runs a quasi-static finite-difference simulation (∇·σ∇V = 0) on a 64×64×36 voxel grid at 1.5 mm resolution. Results update live as you adjust controls.

Skull barrier σ = 0.01 S/m CSF funnel σ = 1.65 S/m 6× focusing ratio SOR solver · 600 iterations
