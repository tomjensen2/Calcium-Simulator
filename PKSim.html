Interactive Pharmacokinetic Simulator

```

This interactive simulator models drug pharmacokinetics in a three-compartment system (depot, extracellular, intracellular) to explore how transporter expression affects therapeutic efficacy.

Control Options (left) let you adjust dosing parameters, disease-specific transport changes, pharmacokinetic parameters, and efficacy thresholds.

The plots show extracellular drug concentration, drug efficacy over time (with therapeutic window), compartmental distribution, and summary metrics comparing normal vs. disease conditions.

Dosing Parameters

Dose (mg) 100
Dosing Interval
Duration (days) 14

Disease Parameters

Transporter Expression (fold) 3.0
Disease Uptake Rate (fold) 1.0
Normal Volume Ratio (IC/EC) 4.0
Disease Volume Ratio (IC/EC) 4.0

PK Parameters

Absorption Rate (1/h) 2.0
Elimination Rate (1/h) 0.5
Basal Uptake Rate (1/h) 0.10

Efficacy Parameters
